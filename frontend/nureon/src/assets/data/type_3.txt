Strengths:
- Self-assured and charming
- Ambitious and competent
- Goal-oriented with a focus on success
- Adaptable and able to excel in varied environments
- Efficient and practical

Weaknesses:
- Can become overly competitive
- May neglect feelings and focus too much on productivity
- Tendency to present a false self to be admired
- Can be impatient and dismissive of those perceived as less driven
- Struggles with work-life balance

About your Enneatype:
Type 3s are known as "The Achievers." They are success-oriented, pragmatic, and image-conscious. Their focus is on achieving goals, excelling in their field, and being admired for their accomplishments. At their best, Type 3s are authentic, inspiring leaders who bring out the best in others. However, they can also struggle with the need for constant validation and may prioritize success over personal relationships or emotional health.

Growth:
- Developing authenticity and recognizing that self-worth is intrinsic, not based on external success.
- Learning to slow down and connect with deeper emotions rather than focusing only on accomplishments.
- Embracing vulnerability and allowing others to see imperfections.

Stress:
- Feeling like a failure or not living up to an ideal image.
- Becoming overly focused on work and neglecting emotional needs.
- Struggling with feelings of inadequacy or impostor syndrome despite outward success.
