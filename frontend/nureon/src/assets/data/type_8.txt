Strengths:
- Self-confident and decisive
- Protective and resourceful
- Strong leadership qualities
- Assertive and action-oriented
- Willing to stand up for themselves and others

Weaknesses:
- Can be confrontational and domineering
- Difficulty expressing vulnerability or softer emotions
- May struggle with control and desire to avoid dependence
- Prone to excessive intensity or aggression
- Impatient and intolerant of weakness in others

About your Enneatype:
Type 8s are known as "The Challengers." They are strong-willed, assertive, and have a natural tendency to take charge in situations. Type 8s are driven by a need to protect themselves and those around them from vulnerability or control by others. While they are often seen as confident and powerful, they may struggle to acknowledge their own vulnerability. At their best, Type 8s are empowering, inspiring leaders who use their strength to protect others. However, their tendency to be overly controlling or confrontational can alienate people or create conflict in their relationships.

Growth:
- Developing openness to vulnerability and emotional sensitivity.
- Practicing patience and tolerance towards others’ weaknesses.
- Letting go of the need to control every situation and allowing for collaboration.

Stress:
- Feeling out of control or vulnerable.
- Reacting with anger or confrontation when feeling threatened.
- Struggling to trust others and allowing for dependence.
