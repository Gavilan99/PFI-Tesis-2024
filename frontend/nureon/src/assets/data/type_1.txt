Title: The Reformer
Core Description:
Principled, purposeful, self-controlled, and perfectionistic.

Strengths:

Strive for improvement
High standards
Strong sense of ethics
Organized and efficient
Conscientious and rational
Weaknesses:

Can be critical of self and others
Prone to resentment when things don’t meet expectations
Struggles with relaxing and allowing imperfection
Tendency towards impatience
Growth:
In growth, Type 1s become more accepting, patient, and spontaneous. They embrace life’s imperfections and develop more self-compassion.

Stress:
Under stress, Type 1s may become more controlling and rigid. They can feel overwhelmed by frustration and become excessively critical.
