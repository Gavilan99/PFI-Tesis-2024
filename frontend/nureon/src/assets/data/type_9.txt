Strengths:
- Calm and peaceful
- Nonjudgmental and accepting of others
- Supportive and empathetic
- Able to see multiple perspectives
- Harmonious and patient

Weaknesses:
- Tendency to avoid conflict at all costs
- Difficulty asserting themselves or expressing personal needs
- May become disengaged or passive-aggressive
- Prone to procrastination and inertia
- Struggles with indecisiveness or self-forgetting

About your Enneatype:
Type 9s are known as "The Peacemakers." They are easygoing, supportive, and driven by a deep desire for inner and outer harmony. Type 9s avoid conflict and seek to create a sense of peace both within themselves and in their relationships. While they are accepting and understanding of others, they often suppress their own desires or opinions to maintain harmony. At their best, Type 9s are mediators who bring people together, but their fear of conflict can lead to complacency or avoidance of important issues.

Growth:
- Learning to assert themselves and express personal opinions or needs.
- Embracing conflict as an opportunity for growth and understanding.
- Staying engaged and focused rather than disengaging to avoid discomfort.

Stress:
- Feeling overwhelmed by conflict or confrontation.
- Becoming disconnected from themselves and their goals.
- Avoiding decision-making or responsibility.
