Title: The Helper
Core Description:
Caring, interpersonal, generous, and people-pleasing.

Strengths:

Empathic and compassionate
Strong desire to help others
Generous and nurturing
Good at building and maintaining relationships
Intuitive about others' needs
Weaknesses:

Can become possessive and overly dependent
Difficulty recognizing their own needs
Prone to people-pleasing and seeking approval
Can feel unappreciated and resentful when their efforts go unnoticed
Growth:
In growth, Type 2s become more self-nurturing and autonomous. They learn to set boundaries and prioritize their own needs, fostering healthier relationships.

Stress:
Under stress, Type 2s may become intrusive and manipulative, feeling hurt when their support is not reciprocated. They can also become overly focused on gaining approval.
