Strengths:
- Creative and expressive
- Emotionally honest and authentic
- Introspective and self-aware
- Sensitive and empathetic to others' emotions
- Able to appreciate beauty and depth in life

Weaknesses:
- Prone to mood swings and melancholy
- Can be self-absorbed and withdrawn
- Tends to compare themselves to others, feeling inadequate
- May struggle with envy and longing for what is missing
- Can dwell in their emotions without finding resolution

About your Enneatype:
Type 4s are known as "The Individualists." They are self-aware, sensitive, and reserved, often feeling different from others and seeking to find their own identity. Type 4s focus on expressing their emotions and finding personal meaning in life. At their best, they are deeply creative and authentic, offering unique perspectives. However, they can also struggle with feelings of inadequacy and envy, longing for a sense of purpose or fulfillment that always seems just out of reach.

Growth:
- Learning to accept and value themselves as they are, without needing to be different or special.
- Embracing both positive and negative emotions without becoming trapped in them.
- Cultivating a sense of gratitude for the present moment and the beauty around them.

Stress:
- Feeling misunderstood or disconnected from others.
- Struggling with self-worth, leading to bouts of melancholy or withdrawal.
- Focusing too much on the past or future, leading to feelings of longing or envy.
