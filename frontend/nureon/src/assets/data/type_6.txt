About your Enneatype:
Type 6s are known as "The Loyalists." They are deeply loyal to their relationships and value security and stability. Type 6s often anticipate potential problems and are always on the lookout for possible dangers, making them excellent problem-solvers. However, they may also experience high levels of anxiety and self-doubt, seeking constant reassurance from others. At their best, they are courageous and stand by their beliefs, showing immense loyalty and commitment. Their desire for safety and stability can sometimes lead to overthinking or indecisiveness.

Growth:
- Learning to trust themselves and their intuition.
- Developing confidence in decision-making without seeking excessive reassurance.
- Balancing their need for security with embracing uncertainty and risk.

Stress:
- Feeling overwhelmed by anxiety and fear of the unknown.
- Becoming paralyzed by indecision or self-doubt.
- Over-reliance on others for validation and reassurance.
