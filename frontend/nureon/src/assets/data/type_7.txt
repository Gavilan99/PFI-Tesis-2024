Strengths:
- Enthusiastic and optimistic
- Spontaneous and adventurous
- Quick to find solutions to problems
- Highly curious and eager to learn
- Adaptable and able to thrive in diverse situations

Weaknesses:
- Difficulty staying focused and committed
- May avoid negative emotions or situations
- Prone to impulsiveness and overindulgence
- Restless and constantly seeking new experiences
- Can become scattered and distracted

About your Enneatype:
Type 7s are known as "The Enthusiasts." They are energetic, fun-loving, and constantly on the lookout for new adventures and experiences. Type 7s have an innate desire to avoid pain and boredom, which often leads them to pursue a wide range of interests. However, this can sometimes make them scattered and unfocused. At their best, they are joyful, spontaneous, and bring positive energy to those around them. Their biggest challenge lies in their tendency to avoid negative emotions, which can prevent them from fully processing or dealing with difficulties.

Growth:
- Developing the ability to face difficult emotions rather than avoiding them.
- Practicing commitment and follow-through in long-term projects or relationships.
- Focusing on depth and presence, rather than seeking constant novelty.

Stress:
- Feeling trapped or limited by commitments.
- Experiencing frustration when reality doesn’t match their idealized visions.
- Struggling with restlessness and a fear of missing out (FOMO).
